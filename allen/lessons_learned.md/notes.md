How has your understanding of what LLMs are useful for in a journalism context changed during this class? 
- At the start of this class, I already had high hopes for what LLMs could do for journalism. I knew that it could be useful in the sense of data extracting, alongside some other tasks that usually would take humans a long time to do. However I had my doubts, this was mostly surrounding parsing documents and the AIs comprehensive ability. Previously I have had token classes where we have tried to get the LLMs to read a lengthy article and it couldn't provide all of the context from that article, in forms that weren't papers. My opinion on that has changed a lot from this class, as AI gets better it surprisingly can provide better responses that journalists could actually use. In the creation of beat books the LLMs showed that they can actually comprehend when working hand by hand with a human. It even sometimes provided a little too much context, but I absolutely see AI as something that could help journalists now in ways that go beyond data extracting. It can actually teach a journalist and show journalists ways in which to see information that would be hard or take long for that journalist to create.


What are the biggest problems you've had using LLMs for the work in this class?
- I think the biggest problem I had with the LLMs in this class is that the smaller models that usually dont train on data don't work as well as the bigger open models. I thought that this problem was particularly more seen in the creation of the second beat book rather than the 1st. Another problem that I seen a lot using LLMs was that the longer you would interact the worse answers you would get which was not ideal for projects that have a lot of push back. Lastly the last problem that was consistent was that the LLMs when given non specific range would talk to me in a way that made me feel less educational, it would over contextualize something a lot of times or state things as a College Student its pretty obvious we should all know. These were three of the problems that were consistent throughout. 

What specific advice would you give to fellow journalism students about using LLMs to understand news stories or archives?
- Some advice I would give would be to make sure your prompts are very specific and make sure you know whatever model you're using limits. This not only would save you a lot of time but it would give you the best answer you are looking for. I would also suggest giving the LLMs within the prompt exaples of what you are looking for. This not only would make the LLms work better but it makes them understand and it gives it a guide. I never recommend giving the LLMs free range, as this only provides answers that you wont be happy with. Ulimately my biggest piece of advice is to always be think of a LLMs as your assistant, someone you have to guide and be in control of sometimes. Never forget that you are in full control.
