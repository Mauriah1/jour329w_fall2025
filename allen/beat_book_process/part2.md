**A Comprehensive Guide to Reporting on Race and Diversity on the Eastern Shore**

As a reporter covering race and diversity on the Eastern Shore, it is essential to understand the complex and evolving landscape of this topic. This guide provides a narrative and chronological analysis of key stories, introducing readers to important people, institutions, and issues.

**Historical Context and Ongoing Struggles**

The Eastern Shore has a rich and complex history, with stories of racial tension and inequality dating back to the late 19th century. The lynching of James Taylor, a 23-year-old Black man, in 1892, is a tragic example of this history. Today, efforts are being made to acknowledge and learn from this past, with initiatives such as the creation of a marker in Taylor's honor and the development of a documentary film, "Get on the Bus," which explores the Civil Rights Movement and its legacy.

**Education and Awareness**

Education and awareness are crucial in addressing issues of race and diversity. The documentary film "Get on the Bus" is an example of how storytelling can be used to educate and raise awareness about the importance of equality and justice. The film follows a group of Eastern Shore residents as they travel to Civil Rights era landmarks, highlighting the ongoing struggle for racial equality and the importance of remembering and learning from the past.

**Hate Crimes and Discrimination**

Hate crimes and discrimination remain a significant concern on the Eastern Shore. A recent example is the case of three 13-year-old students at Plum Point Middle School, who were charged with harassment and hate crimes for displaying swastikas and making Nazi salutes. This incident highlights the need for continued education and awareness, as well as the importance of addressing and preventing hate crimes.

**Voting Rights and Representation**

Voting rights and representation are critical issues on the Eastern Shore, particularly in communities with significant Black populations. The town of Federalsburg recently settled a lawsuit with the NAACP and the Caucus of African American Leaders, agreeing to change its election system to ensure more representation of Black residents. This settlement is a significant step forward, but ongoing efforts are needed to ensure that all communities have equal access to representation and voting rights.

**Key Players and Institutions**

Several key players and institutions are working to address issues of race and diversity on the Eastern Shore. These include:

* The NAACP, which is working to promote voting rights and representation in communities of color
* The Caucus of African American Leaders, which is advocating for policies and initiatives that support Black communities
* The Calvert County State's Attorney's Office, which is working to address hate crimes and discrimination
* The town of Federalsburg, which is implementing changes to its election system and working to promote diversity and inclusion

**Conclusion**

Reporting on race and diversity on the Eastern Shore requires a deep understanding of the complex history and ongoing struggles of this region.
