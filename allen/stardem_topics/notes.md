Star-Democrat Topic Classification 10/02/25

Approach Selection: 
I chose Option 2 because it gave me more control over the topic categories. After exploring the data with the embedding atlas and looking at sample articles, I had formed hypotheses about the main topics covered by the Star-Democrat. I wanted to see if my initial assessment aligned with what the LLM would find when systematically classifying all 200 stories.

My Topic List:
- Education
- Religion
- Animals
- Historical Information
- Sports
- Local Government
- Elections
- Courts and Public Safety
- Business and Economy
- Farming and Agriculture
- Other

Reasoning:
I selected these topics based on the distribution of stories in the sample data. While common topics like Local Government and Education were well-represented, I also noticed significant coverage of more specialized topics such as Historical Information and Animals, which reflected the publication's unique editorial focus on Eastern Shore culture and community.

Results Analysis: 
The LLM performed fairly well at assigning stories to the topics I designated. I was particularly surprised by the Historical Information section, which contained 24 stories. While this was a very niche topic, the substantial coverage reflects the Star-Democrat's focus on Eastern Shore heritage. I was also surprised by the "Other" section as this had 32 stories, as I noticed it included many community news stories. I initally realized I had forgotten to include a "Community News" tag in my original list, but I was pleased to see that the "Other" category captured most of these stories appropriately. I was also satisfied that each topic I assigned had at least 5 or more stories, which met my expectations for a balanced distribution.

The stories were generally well-categorized. For instance, the election stories were genuinely about election news, such as coverage of conservative Latino voters' reactions to Trump's return to office. The sports section also contained relevant sports content, though it focused heavily on local sports, which I think could benefit from more specific categorization in the future. Overall, I could have been clearer in distinguishing between local government news and general local community news, in order to eliminate the other section but overall I liked the responses I was given.

I chose Option 2 because I felt it was more collaborative with the LLM. I feel that if I had given the LLM complete freedom, it might have generated generic or overly broad categories. I really appreciated having the ability to set clear parameters and having the LLM match stories to those specific topics. In my experience with giving LLMs full control, I often find myself needing to edit extensively afterward, and this approach eliminated that extra work. What surprised me most was how responsive and accurate the LLM was when working within my defined framework.
